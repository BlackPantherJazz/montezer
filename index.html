Inside the pedal

  1. Input and loudness

    The guitar signal is split three ways: a dry copy, a copy for the talk-box path, and a copy for analysis. Every animation frame the code reads 2048 samples and measures their loudness in dBFS (RMS). Anything quieter than the Gate knob counts as silence, so string noise doesn't make the singer mumble.

    find it: tick()

  2. Pitch tracker (YIN)

    The samples are halved in rate, then compared against delayed copies of themselves. The delay where the wave best matches itself is one period, and frequency is the sample rate divided by that period. YIN normalizes this difference, takes the first dip below 0.15, then interpolates between samples for sub-cent accuracy. The whole window is about 43 ms, which is why a low B on bass takes a beat to lock in.

    find it: yin() · YIN_THRESHOLD

  3. Vocal cords (the source)

    A custom PeriodicWave plays at the tracked pitch. Its harmonics fall off as 1/n1.1, close to a real glottal buzz. Vibrato is a 5 Hz LFO on the oscillator's detune, held back for 180 ms after each note so it blooms the way a singer's does. Choir stacks up to five slightly detuned voices; Breath mixes in filtered noise.

    find it: buildVoices() · noteOn() · glottalWave

  4. Vocal tract (the filter)

    Four bandpass filters sit at F1 to F4 and are summed in parallel. Moving the vowel dot retunes them. Throat scales every formant together: a shorter vocal tract resonates higher, which is most of the difference between a bass and a soprano. Mouth uses your picking dynamics to open the vowel from "oo" toward your chosen vowel, like a wah that talks. In talk-box mode the guitar itself is distorted and fed through the same filters.

    find it: applyFormants() · vowelPoint()

  5. Output

    Blend mixes dry guitar with the voice. A fast compressor acts as a limiter so a loud pluck can't clip, and a second analyser feeds the spectrum display.

    find it: initAudio()

Try this

  • EarsStart the bass demo, then switch Octave to 0. The voice drops to 58 Hz and turns into a growl. Real singers rarely go that low, which is why bass needs an octave up.
  • EarsSlide the dot straight down from "ee" to "a". Only F1 moves, and you hear the jaw open. Slide across from "ee" to "oo" and only F2 moves.
  • EarsSet Throat to soprano and Choir to five voices. That's most of a choir pad.
  • EarsUpload a recording of chords and compare Singer with Talk box. Singer follows one pitch; Talk box keeps every note because it filters your actual strings.
  • CodeChange YIN_THRESHOLD from 0.15 to 0.4 and watch CONF and the note jump between octaves.
  • CodeChange GLOTTAL_ROLLOFF to 2.0 for a dark, covered voice, or 0.5 for a buzzy kazoo.
  • CodeAdd a fifth formant at 4500 Hz to FORMANT_BW and FORMANT_GAIN and listen for extra "air".
Live input only works when you open the downloaded file in Chrome on your computer, because the claude.ai viewer blocks the microphone. Use headphones so the voice doesn't feed back into your pickups.
